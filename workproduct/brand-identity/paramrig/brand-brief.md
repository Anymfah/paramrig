# ParamRig — Working Brand Brief

Status: draft. Product principles and the founder's aesthetic preference are
confirmed; proposed creative interpretations still need validation.

## Purpose

Let AI build the tools. Keep creation human.

## Audience and value

Creative developers, vibecoders, and technical artists. AI builds custom
controls and bindings; humans shape the result and retain creative authorship.
The intended scope includes interfaces, SVG, logos, animation, 3D, procedural
systems, and proofs of concept, without a closed catalogue of presets.

## Personality

Confirmed by the founder: understated, modern, minimal.
Proposed complementary traits: precise and approachable.
Understated should not mean anonymous; technical should not mean intimidating.

## Positioning and essence

Working positioning: an open-source workbench for human-tuning AI-built systems.
Proposed essence: Human precision.
Exclusive market claims remain unverified and must not be presented as facts.

## Evidence and open questions

The founder reports building custom editors across existing projects to achieve
visual precision. These are motivation, not proof of ParamRig implementation.
Competitive visual research, distinctiveness, trademark checks, and mark design
remain pending. The founder reports purchasing paramrig.com.

## Boundaries

English is the canonical brand and product language. Identity work stays inside
ParamRig. ANYM, Helios, and Stellary remain untouched. This brief does not select
a final logo, palette, typography, website design, or application interface.

## Founder feedback — first mark exploration

- Reject Pivot: the founder sees too much resemblance to Steam.
- Develop Modular P further; its initial construction is too basic and unrefined.
- Add considered structural complexity while keeping clean, precise geometry.
- Reject the first wordmark typography; explore more distinctive drawn lettering.
- No final symbol, typeface, or palette has been approved.

## Founder feedback — modular studies

- The second-round lettering moves in the wrong direction and is rejected.
- Contour is the most promising P exploration, but is not approved.
- Next exploration: refine Contour and evaluate lettering separately.
- Avoid treating a tentative preference as a final identity decision.

## Founder feedback — concept reset

- Restore the first exploration's lettering as the preferred reference.
- Reject the outlined Contour family entirely.
- Seek simpler silhouettes with richer underlying ideas and double readings.
- Conceptual depth must not be confused with extra lines or ornament.
- No final mark is approved; the earlier Contour preference is superseded.

## Founder shortlist — Agency and Coform

- The founder enthusiastically selected Agency and Coform for refinement.
- Agency: the negative-space cursor shapes the solid P.
- Coform: two complementary masses reveal a P between them.
- Preserve the first exploration's lettering and monochrome treatment.
- Refine both existing concepts; do not invent another direction.
- Neither shortlisted concept is the final winner yet.

## Current preference

The founder slightly prefers Coform, describing it as more memorable, while
still liking Agency strongly. Coform is the leading candidate, not yet a final
approved logo. Memorability is the founder's impression, not a tested result.

## Selected direction and vector handoff

The founder subsequently confirmed Coform and requested a pixel-accurate SVG.
Coform is now the selected direction; Agency remains an archived alternative.
The symbol and original stacked wordmark have been reconstructed as vector paths
in `assets/brand/`. Source reference, calibrated measurements and visual proof
are retained alongside this brief. The deliverable is a measured subpixel-close
reconstruction, not a claim of identical raster pixels or original vector data.

## Optical cleanup after vector acceptance

The founder accepted the vector reconstruction, then requested removal of its
small irregularities. The current master prioritizes clean geometry over tracing
raster noise: long regular curves, exact straight segments and aligned tangents.
The original wordmark is lightly smoothed without font substitution. The first
SVGs are retained in `reference/svg-v1/`; before/after proof is in `cleanup-qa/`.

## Wordmark correction after founder review

The founder rejected the merely smoothed lettering as buggy and dirty. A new
custom vector wordmark replaces raster-derived contours with explicit straight
stems and regular curves. Repeated a glyphs are identical; P/R share their upper
bowl construction and the i dot is circular. The general sans-serif character,
single-storey a/g, word width and placement are retained, with squarer terminals.
The accepted Coform symbol is byte-for-byte unchanged. The previous smoothed
version is archived in `reference/svg-v2/`; current visual proof is in
`wordmark-qa/`. The new wordmark has not yet been accepted by the founder.
No application font, palette, website, or other project is changed.

## Final lettering detail pass

The founder said the redrawn lettering was better and requested one last pass.
Changes stay local: matching inner arches on m, consistent round bowls on a/g,
and a slight adjustment to the R leg's inner edge. Existing spacing, silhouette,
dimensions and Coform symbol are preserved. The preceding lettering is saved
in `reference/svg-v3/`; proof is in `wordmark-polish-qa/`. Final founder approval
is still pending; no new font, color direction or application work was added.

## Logo acceptance and first branding board

The founder accepted the final lettering refinement and requested a branding
board. The symbol and wordmark are now the approved geometric masters. The
board embeds them unchanged and proposes Carbon/Chalk/Stone/Signal colors,
Manrope for copy and IBM Plex Mono for parameter labels. All board copy is in
English. Horizontal arrangements, messaging, palette, typography and static
editor/social applications remain proposals. No site or application was built,
and no other repository was modified.

## Monochrome revision

The founder rejected the citron accent and agreed to restart from the
Carbon/Chalk foundation. Board 02 removes Signal throughout, keeping Stone as
the supporting neutral. Logo geometry, lettering, fonts and overall composition
are unchanged. No replacement color is chosen yet; any accent study comes after
reviewing this neutral foundation. The prior board is archived as
`reference/branding-board-01/`. The new export is pending founder review.

## Supporting typography reset

The founder explicitly rejected IBM Plex Mono and the familiar AI/dev typography
register. Do not re-propose IBM Plex Mono or add a monospace by default. Manrope
is also unapproved and remains open for replacement. The custom logo lettering
is unchanged and approved.

A separate comparison now tests Uncut Sans and Familjen Grotesk at identical
sizes and weights across headlines, prose and parameters, without monospace.
These are aesthetic candidates, not claims of never being used by AI. No
candidate is chosen yet; previous board typography must not be treated as a
current specification. Only ParamRig-local design artifacts were touched.

## Apple reference and restrained typography study

The founder rejected both Uncut Sans and Familjen Grotesk as too familiar, then
named Apple as the reference. The agreed interpretation is sober, precise,
unobtrusive typography with hierarchy carried by size, weight and spacing.
It is not an instruction to imitate Apple branding or redistribute Apple fonts.

Board 03 proposes Public Sans 400/600 for all supporting text and tabular
parameter values, without monospace. Technical uppercase micro-labels are
replaced by sentence case, the main message has more breathing room, and the
extra hero symbol is removed. The approved three logo masters and the neutral
palette remain unchanged. Board 02 is archived in `reference/branding-board-02/`.
Public Sans is still a proposal, not a founder-approved typeface. The board is
static artwork only; no website, app or other repository was modified.
