# Repository rules

## Commit authorship

- Never add AI signatures, AI attribution, or assistant branding to commit
  subjects, bodies, or trailers.
- Do not add phrases such as "Generated by", "Created with", or "Assisted by"
  followed by an AI tool, model, or assistant name.
- Never add an AI assistant, model, tool, or bot as a `Co-authored-by` entry.
- Preserve the contributor's configured Git identity. Do not invent an author,
  change Git identity, or disable cryptographic signing to satisfy this rule.
- Legitimate human co-author credits and cryptographic commit signatures are
  allowed. Technical discussion of AI functionality is also allowed: this rule
  prohibits attribution footers, not descriptions of the product.
- Before committing, inspect the complete commit message for these violations.

## Language and scope

- Use English for product UI, documentation, APIs, error messages, and commits.
- Keep work inside ParamRig. Do not modify ANYM, Stellary, or Helios repositories.
- Preserve the approved Coform symbol and custom wordmark unless a change is
  explicitly requested.
- Do not commit secrets, local environment files, caches, or internal discovery
  session data.
